
=== Study 21: ESD gravitational-wave derivation (vacuum-tensor applicability) ===

  Second companion to Study 19.  Axioms (A1)-(A3) applied to GWs.
  A gravitational wave is a tensor perturbation h_{mu nu} of the
  background metric, propagating at c in vacuum via the
  linearized Einstein equations.  GW is NOT a localized
  massive subsystem (A1 fails) and has NO proper acceleration
  of an associated mass (A2 fails).  R(u) cannot dress
  the propagation.

  Consequences (this study tests them):
    * c_GW = c exactly (matches GW170817 to < 3e-15)
    * Two tensor polarizations only (no scalar/vector modes)
    * GW amplitude h ~ 1/D_L with LambdaCDM D_L (from Study 20)
    * Standard-siren H_0 = LambdaCDM analysis result
