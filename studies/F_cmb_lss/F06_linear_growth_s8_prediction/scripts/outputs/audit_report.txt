
=== Study 19: ESD linear-growth derivation + S_8 prediction ===

  Derivation: R(u) = s/Sigma(u) acts on localized subsystems
  with a well-defined g against a separated spectator (Axioms
  A1, A2 of Paper 1).  Linear cosmological perturbations
  delta(x,t) are fluctuations of the SAME field as the
  background; no system/spectator split exists, so axiom (A1)
  fails.  Therefore R(u) does NOT modify the linear growth
  equation, and sigma_8 (a strictly linear quantity) inherits
  the standard LambdaCDM value.

  When a mode goes nonlinear and forms a virialized halo,
  (A1) is restored and R(u) applies as in Studies 09-16.
