# Study 17 paper notes

Null test: ESD reproduces GR Schwarzschild photon-ring diameters
for M87* (39.70 μas) and Sgr A* (51.51 μas) to machine precision.
Closure-pool correction R(u) at the photon sphere is ~3e-21 (M87*)
and ~2e-26 (Sgr A*). EHT measurements consistent with ESD/GR within
0.77σ and 0.13σ respectively. Confirms IR-only nature of closure-pool
kernel: no spurious modification at strong-field scales.
