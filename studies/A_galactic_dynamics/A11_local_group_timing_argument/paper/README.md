# Study A11 paper notes

ESD timing-argument extension. Under R(u) at low-acceleration LG
scales, the timing argument's mass requirement is the *baryonic*
mass, not the dynamical mass. Backwards-integration shooting solves
for M_b giving r(t=0)=0 starting from today's (r, dot r).

Result: ESD M_b ~ 1.5e11 Msun, matching the observed LG baryon
budget; Newton M_dyn ~ 4.5e12 Msun matches the LCDM dynamical
demand. The factor ~30 is the dark-matter content under LCDM.
